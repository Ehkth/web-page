01 · Infographic

Stop bullying, start respect

Bullying is repeated hurtful behaviour. Learn to recognise it and to answer with respect.

✋

Physical

Hitting, pushing or kicking.

🗣️

Verbal

Name-calling, insults or laughing at someone.

👥

Social

Leaving someone out on purpose.

🌐

Online

Sending or sharing hurtful messages.

What can we do?

💛Be kind.
🤝Include others.
🚫Don't share hurtful messages.
🙋Ask a trusted adult.
💛
Key message: Everyone deserves respect, safety and belonging. Every child has the right to feel safe and included at school.

06 · Scenarios

What would you do?

Read the situation, think of a respectful answer and then compare it with the suggestion.

🙋

A new classmate

A new student is sitting alone at break. What can you say?

“Would you like to play with us?”

Invite the person to join. A simple invitation can change their day.
📱

A hurtful online message

A class group shares a mean message about someone. What should you do?

Do not forward it. Save evidence if it is safe and tell a trusted adult.

Do not send the message to other people. Ask an adult for help.
🍽️

Left out at lunch

You see a classmate eating alone. What can you do?

“Come and sit with us!”

Offer a place at your table. Inclusion starts with a small gesture.
💭

You made a mistake

You said something unkind and your classmate feels sad. What can you say?

“I am sorry. I will not say that again.”

Saying sorry and changing your behaviour is also a form of respect.

10 · Teacher notes

Teacher notes and learning approach

Pedagogical guidance to adapt this page to your primary school group.

Pedagogical approach

This page connects English practice with the student's real context. It draws on the principles of the post-method approach: practical communication, participation and relevance.

Universal Design for Learning (UDL) is reflected in short text, visual organisation, oral modelling, written or spoken answer options and constant teacher support.

Suggested assessment

  • Reading comprehension in the short situations.
  • Choosing safe and respectful answers.
  • Use of the target vocabulary (respect, include, kind...).
  • One inclusive proposal, spoken or written.

Adaptations

  • Adjust the length of the readings and the time for each activity.
  • Read out loud and allow spoken answers or drawings.
  • Use the browser audio or record your own voice.
  • Check the video and external links before class.

Privacy and safety

No personal data is requested. Reflection and exit-ticket answers are saved only in the student's browser (localStorage).

AI tools are used under supervision and always have a no-technology alternative.
